McNeilus Maranatha Christian College (An Adventist Center of Higher Learning), formerly known as Maranatha Christian College - MCC, is a Seventh-day Adventist Christian school in Kalaymyo, Myanmar (Burma). It is owned and operated by Myanmar Union Conference of Seventh-day Adventists. It was established in 2000 and is a part of the Seventh-day Adventist education system, the world's second largest Christian school system. Location MMCC is located on Khaing Shwe Waa Rd, Chamie Aungshii, PO 02091, Kalaymyo, Sagaing Division, Myanmar.
