Wind turbines are amazing machines that generate electricity from the wind! Here's how they work in simple terms:

**Main Parts:**

1. **Rotor Blades:** These are the long, thin wings that spin around when the wind blows.
2. **Hub:** This is the central part where the blades attach.
3. **Generator:** This is the part that converts the spinning energy into electricity.

**How it Works:**

1. **Wind Blows:** When the wind blows, it makes the rotor blades spin around the hub.
2. **Energy Generation:** As the blades spin, they create a force called torque, which drives the generator to produce electricity.
3. **Electricity Generation:** The generator uses the spinning energy to generate alternating current (AC) electricity.
4. **Power Transmission:** The generated electricity is transmitted through cables to a power grid or used locally.

**Key Points:**

* Wind turbines work best when the wind blows steadily and consistently, like in open areas with minimal obstacles.
* The rotor blades are designed to capture the kinetic energy of the wind and convert it into mechanical energy, which then generates electricity.
* Modern wind turbines can generate enough electricity for hundreds or even thousands of homes!

That's a basic overview of how wind turbines work!
