Wind turbines are amazing machines that generate electricity from the wind! Here's how they work in simple terms:

**Main Parts:**

1. **Rotor Blades:** These are the long, thin wings that spin around when the wind blows.
2. **Hub:** This is the central part where the rotor blades attach.
3. **Generator:** This is the part that converts the spinning energy into electricity.

**How it Works:**

1. **Wind Blows:** When the wind blows, it makes the rotor blades spin around the hub.
2. **Energy Generation:** As the blades spin, they create a force called torque, which drives the generator to produce electricity.
3. **Electricity Generation:** The generator uses the spinning energy to generate an alternating current (AC) electricity.
4. **Power Transmission:** The generated electricity is transmitted through cables to a substation, where it's transformed into high-voltage electricity that can be sent to homes and businesses.

**Key Points:**

* Wind turbines work best when the wind blows steadily at speeds of around 10-25 miles per hour (16-40 kilometers per hour).
* The rotor blades are designed to capture the energy in the wind and convert it into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.
* Modern wind turbines can generate enough electricity to power hundreds or even thousands of homes!

That's a basic overview of how wind turbines work!
