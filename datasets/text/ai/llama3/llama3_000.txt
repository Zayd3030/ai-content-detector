Wind turbines are amazing machines that generate electricity from the wind! Here's how they work in simple terms:

**Main Parts:**

1. **Rotor Blades:** These are the long, thin wings that spin around when the wind blows.
2. **Hub:** This is the central part where the rotor blades attach.
3. **Generator:** This is the part that converts the spinning motion into electricity.

**How it Works:**

1. **Wind Blows:** When the wind blows, it makes the rotor blades spin around the hub.
2. **Energy Generation:** As the blades spin, they create a force called lift, which generates energy.
3. **Generator Spins:** The spinning blades make the generator spin too!
4. **Electricity Produced:** The generator converts the spinning motion into electricity, which is then sent to the power grid.

**Key Points:**

* Wind turbines only work when the wind blows at a certain speed (usually around 7-10 mph).
* As the turbine spins faster, it generates more electricity.
* Modern wind turbines are designed to be efficient and quiet, with some even having built-in noise-reducing features!

That's the basic idea! Wind turbines harness the power of the wind to generate clean energy.
