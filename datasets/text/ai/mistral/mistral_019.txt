Cybersecurity threats refer to malicious activities that aim to compromise the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of digital information or systems. These threats can take various forms, including viruses, malware, ransomware, phishing attacks, and denial-of-service (DoS) attacks. Hackers often exploit vulnerabilities in software, hardware, or human behavior to gain unauthorized access to sensitive data or disrupt the normal functioning of a system. The impact of these threats can be severe, leading to financial loss, damage to reputation, and potential harm to individuals' privacy. As technology continues to evolve, so do cybersecurity threats, making it crucial for organizations and individuals to implement robust security measures to protect against these risks.
